Data Description

  1. Identify your data source.

The Haupt Lab and MARINe/PISCO dataset

  1. Describe your data, including variables and data types.

List of variables in the dataframe:

Site: character

Date: numeric

Transect #: numeric

Latitude and Longitude for start and end transect: numeric

Quadrat #: numeric

Sea or Land Side: character

One column for the percent cover each organism observed: includes various functional groups of algae, kelp, mussels, anemones, barnacles, and other: numeric

Urchin density: numeric

Who entered the Data: character

Notes: character

These datasets include the observed percent cover of various organisms within intertidal sites across the Monterey Peninsula

  1. Identify the research questions you want to answer.

How have both urchin density and algae cover changed over time and how do they appear to have interacted with each other? Has one increased and the other decreased, or vice versa?
